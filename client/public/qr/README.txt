J-Pop Corner QR Codes Folder
============================
Place your payment QR code images here with any of these filenames:

For GCash:
  gcash.png   or   gcash.jpg   or   gcash.jpeg

For Maya:
  maya.png    or   maya.jpg    or   maya.jpeg

For InstaPay (Maribank / Any bank):
  instapay.png   or   instapay.jpg   or   instapay.jpeg

The application will automatically detect and fetch these QR codes during customer checkout and in the Admin Desk!
